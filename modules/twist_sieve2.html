WHAT'S A SIEVE?

A sieve keeps some things and lets the rest fall through. This one keeps the prime numbers.

Every number that isn't prime is a multiple of a smaller prime. So: take the smallest prime, switch off all its multiples. Take the next number still on: nothing smaller divides it, so it's prime. Switch off its multiples. Keep going.

Their corridor has a motion sensor for every number. The prime sensors are old and blind: when only they are left on, we can walk straight through.
