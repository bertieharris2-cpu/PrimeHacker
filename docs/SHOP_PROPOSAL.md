# Shop proposal (Round 27)

**Approved: A, B and D (Round 28), and built.** Atlas Prime's hidden sum is now £3,000,000. The £250,000 items are now £150,000 and the £100,000 items are now £75,000. Extra runs in a mission pay half the fee. The £1,000,000 top items are unchanged.

Nothing in the shop has changed yet. This compares what pupils earned before the finder's fee with what they earn now, and suggests changes.

## What a mission paid before
The vault paid out per lock (random bands), then a speed share (40% to 100%), plus the intel and getaway bonuses.

| Level | One mission, slowest | One mission, fastest |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | about £4,600 | about £24,700 |
| L2 | about £46,000 | about £247,000 |
| L3 | about £460,000 | about £2,470,000 |

## What a mission pays now (the finder's fee, plus up to 15% extras)
| Level | Missions 1–3 | Finale: return the funds | Finale: take it all | Whole level (returning) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 (10%) | £24,000 · £30,000 · £42,000 | £50,000 | £500,000 | £146,000 |
| L2 (15%) | £72,000 · £54,000 · £90,000 | £108,000 | £720,000 | £324,000 |
| L3 (20%) | £300,000 · £240,000 · £180,000 | £1,600,000 | £8,000,000 | £2,320,000 |

**In short:**
- L1 pupils earn about twice what they used to.
- L2 pupils earn about half.
- L3 pupils earn about a sixth.

## The shop today
- 32 items in 6 categories, from £5,000 to £1,000,000.
- Everything together costs about £6,160,000.
- In each category the top item is £1,000,000 and the second is £250,000.

## The problems
1. **The L3 "take it all" (£8,000,000) buys the whole shop in one go.** That breaks the shop, and it makes taking it all the obvious choice for anyone who wants items.
2. **L1 finishes the level with about £146,000.** That buys the cheap items (£5,000–£50,000) but nothing above £100,000. That fits "the best items need higher levels", but L1 pupils may never see a big purchase.
3. **Extra runs in the same mission also pay a fee.** A pupil could keep re-running to farm money.

## Proposed changes (pick any)
- **A. Cap the L3 finale pot.** Atlas Prime's hidden sum goes from £8,000,000 to £3,000,000:
  - return the funds: a £600,000 fee;
  - take it all: £3,000,000, which buys about half the shop.
  - The story still works ("the money each company passed up").
- **B. Cheaper middle tier.** Lower the £250,000 items to £150,000 and the £100,000 items to £75,000, so an L2 level (about £324,000) buys two or three good items.
- **C. Top items stay at £1,000,000.** They stay L3 goals.
- **D. Extra runs pay half the fee** (the mission's own run pays in full).
- **E. Leave everything as it is** and see how it feels in class first.

My recommendation: **A + B + D**.
