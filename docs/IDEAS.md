# Ideas for each part of PRIMENET

Suggestions only. Nothing here is built yet. Effort is a rough guide: **S** is an afternoon, **M** a day or two, **L** a bigger build.

## Across the whole game

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **One continuous heist.** The target bank is chosen at the start and named on every screen. The Blueprint *is* that bank's building, and Prime Hack breaks into the same bank | M |
| **A handler.** A named voice (e.g. ORACLE) sends short comms messages at key moments: "Nice work, KESTREL. Floor two is solid. One more." Typed out with a crackle sound | M |
| **Agent ID card** on the title screen: codename, rank, clearance, vaults breached and a generated agent number, updated after each mission | S |
| **Glitch transitions** between stages: a quick screen tear and static sound, so moving between games feels like jumping systems | S |
| **Badges** for specific achievements: *Square Hunter* (found a square factor pair), *Clean Sweep* (no wrong answers), *Speed Demon*, *Prime Sniper* (every lock first try) | M |
| **Settings screen** for students: text size, reduced motion, sound and a dyslexia-friendly font | M |

## Title screen and briefing

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Mission briefing** after Start: the handler names the target and the plan ("rebuild the blueprint, then get us inside") before the Frequency Scan | S |
| **Identity scan**: hold SPACE while a fingerprint fills to log in. A small physical moment before the maths | S |
| **Target map**: choose which bank to hit from a city map. Harder banks match higher levels | L |

## Prime Frequency Scan

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Show the mistakes** after two misses: wrongly tagged numbers flash red, missed primes pulse | S |
| **Bands that match the level**: L1 scans 1–30, L2 30–70, L3 70–150, with a different band each time | S |
| **Oscilloscope version**: numbers drift across a radar screen; tag the stable (prime) signals before they leave the screen | L |
| **Jammer tool (a sieve)**: click a prime to knock out all its multiples, like the Sieve of Eratosthenes, as a way to clear noise | M |
| **Signal strength**: a faster, cleaner scan carries a bonus into the vault stages | S |

## Factor Vault

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Nudge after two misses** (rescued from the old Factors mission): "4 works. What's its partner?" | S |
| **Pairs-remaining scanner**: shows how many pairs are left, and whether a square pair exists | S |
| **Security camera sweep**: a camera cone passes over the vault; answering while it's watching makes the alarm tick up. Tension without failure | M |
| **Reinforced floors**: square numbers glow differently, with a note on why (one pair repeats) | S |
| **Drag to build**: drag out a rectangle on a grid to make each array, instead of typing, as an option for younger students | L |

## Vault Grid

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Name the rooms**: 1×24 becomes a corridor, 4×6 an office, 2×12 a service tunnel, so the floor plan reads like a real building | S |
| **Place the rooms yourself**: a tiling puzzle to fit every factor pair onto the floor | L |
| **Drone flythrough** of the 3D floor once it rises | M |

## Bank Blueprint

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Hidden room**: if all three floor numbers share a factor, a secret vault room appears ("Every floor divides by 6"). This teaches the highest common factor | M |
| **Weak point**: the floor with the fewest factor pairs glows as the way in, and leads into Prime Hack | S |
| **Plotter print**: the blueprint draws itself line by line on blueprint paper before turning 3D | M |
| **Explore it**: drag to rotate, click a floor to see its factor pairs | M |

## Prime Hack

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Combination lock on screen**: each correct prime turns a tumbler with a click | M |
| **`TRY 7`** command: shows 221 ÷ 7 = 31.57, not a whole number, so trial division becomes a tool | S |
| **Trace meter**: wrong answers raise a trace level; if it fills, an alarm and a new lock. Stakes without a game over | M |
| **Streak bonus**: first-try locks in a row multiply the money (×1.5, ×2) | S |
| **Three-prime locks at L3**: N = p × q × r for the top level | M |

## Finale and rewards

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Finales that match the stage**: a laser-grid finale for the factor half, the vault door for the prime half, a server blackout for a bonus | L |
| **Getaway**: after the vault opens, three rapid "prime or not?" questions to escape before the trace completes | M |
| **Loot shop**: spend the wallet on cosmetics such as terminal colours, door skins and badge frames | L |
| **Most Wanted poster**: the class's top agents from your record files, shown on the board | M |

## Roleplay terminal

| Idea | Effort |
|---|---|
| **Teacher puppet commands**: secret commands trigger an alarm or a message on a student's screen during a drama lesson | M |
| **Two-player mode**: one student hacks, another plays the security admin typing countermeasures | L |
| **DOSSIER command**: a random mission scenario to act out or write about | S |
| **Sound board**: SIREN, GLITCH, TYPEWRITER, BREACH buttons for live performances | S |
